# KayKit Forest Nature Pack 1.0 FREE

This directory contains a small reviewed subset of the free KayKit Forest
Nature Pack. The source pack was downloaded from the author's official itch.io
page:

- https://kaylousberg.itch.io/kaykit-forest
- source archive SHA-256:
  `2ee83e63bb7695f2d884ec27ddf6fce020789a452e7d5c5b0bbdfc4f6ea1fc8c`

The selected glTF files keep their matching `.bin` buffer and the shared
`forest_texture.png`. `LICENSE.txt` is copied verbatim from the archive; the
pack is CC0.

Only grass and rock models from the FREE tier are staged here for visual and
import evaluation. KayKit's modular terrain is not part of the free download;
it is available in the EXTRA tier, while the organized `.blend` source is in
the SOURCE tier. Do not approximate or claim those paid assets from preview
images.

These models may decorate fixed tile prefabs, but they do not change the tile
edge grammar. Any future terrain derived from the paid modular pieces must be
rebuilt in Blender against the project's canonical tile boundary template and
must pass the WATER, LAND, and EMPTY seam checks before promotion.
